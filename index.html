About the fingering

Major and minor scales use the standard fingerings taught in piano scale books. Modes have no single standard, so they follow the same rules: the thumb plays only white keys, it passes under just after a black key wherever it can, and a scale that starts on a white key starts on the thumb in the right hand and ends on it in the left.

Melodic minor raises its 6th and 7th going up and comes back down as natural minor. In F♯ and C♯ minor that moves the right hand’s thumb, so those two scales change fingering on the way down.
